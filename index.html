About Me

As a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the University of Virginia, I focus on multimodal deep learning and computer vision systems that solve practical problems in agriculture and environmental monitoring. I bridge academic research and applied engineering to deliver datasets, models, and tooling that are ready for deployment.

What I do

  • Design and build multimodal datasets for satellite imagery and structured knowledge.
  • Develop vision-language models, segmentation architectures, and robust evaluation pipelines.
  • Translate academic research into reproducible systems for industry and government use.

Impact focus

  • Irrigation classification and agricultural water management.
  • National-scale remote sensing benchmarks.
  • Explainable geospatial AI for decision support.
