NeurIPS 2025

IRRISIGHT

Large-scale multimodal remote-sensing benchmark for irrigation mapping fusing Sentinel-2 imagery, vegetation indices, soil/land/crop metadata, and vision-language prompts.

  • 1.4M+ ML-ready patches across 20+ U.S. states, released on Hugging Face for the research community.
  • +58 IoU improvement moving from RGB-only ResNet (35.2 IoU) to multimodal vision-language models (CLIP / RemoteCLIP at 90+ IoU).
  • Best multimodal model (KIIM): 93.6 IoU · 95.8 Accuracy · 94.6 F1 on held-out states.
  • Built supervised + semi-supervised (pseudo-labeling, weak supervision, teacher–student) pipelines.
