NeurIPS 2025 · Datasets & Benchmarks

IRRISIGHT

Large-scale multimodal remote-sensing benchmark for irrigation mapping fusing Sentinel-2 imagery, vegetation indices, soil/land/crop metadata, and vision-language prompts.

  • 1.4M+ ML-ready patches across 20+ U.S. states; released on Hugging Face.
  • +58 IoU lift moving from RGB-only ResNet (35.2 IoU) to multimodal VLMs (CLIP / RemoteCLIP at 90+ IoU).
  • Best multimodal model (KIIM): 93.6 IoU · 95.8 Acc · 94.6 F1.
  • Supervised + semi-supervised pipelines (pseudo-labeling, weak supervision, teacher–student).

IJCAI 2026 · Lead Author

AG-STELLA

Knowledge-guided spatio-temporal transformer for agricultural land-use activity mapping using AlphaEarth embeddings, Prithvi MAE tokenization, and hydroclimatic priors.

  • Evaluated across 3 U.S. states (UT, CO, WA) on 21K+ labeled samples.
  • +16% macro accuracy and +27% pasture F1 over strong vision and foundation-model baselines.
  • Introduces Temporal Transition Latent Space (TTLSP) and Temporal Feature Fusion (TFF).
  • Stronger cross-state generalization — directly relevant to large-scale model transfer.

IJCAI 2025

KIIM — Knowledge-Informed Irrigation Mapping

Knowledge-informed deep learning for irrigation type mapping from remote sensing — the multimodal model that anchors the IRRISIGHT benchmark.

  • Fuses RGB + crop type + land cover priors for irrigation type classification.
  • Achieves 93.6 IoU · 95.8 Accuracy · 94.6 F1, the top result on IRRISIGHT.
  • Demonstrates the value of knowledge-informed multimodal fusion at production scale.

WACV 2026

PRISM-CAFO

Prior-conditioned, infrastructure-first remote-sensing pipeline for explainable CAFO detection — combining YOLO detection, SAM segmentation, and multimodal fusion.

  • Pipeline: YOLO detection → SAM segmentation → descriptor extraction → multimodal classification → explainability.
  • Improves interpretability and cross-region robustness for large-scale livestock monitoring.
  • Co-authored with the UVA Biocomplexity Institute team.

KDD 2025

IrrMap

National-scale irrigation method mapping benchmark with reproducible, distributed data and training pipelines built on top of large geospatial sources.

  • Standardized end-to-end processing from raw geospatial sources to ML-ready samples at U.S. scale.
  • Published baselines spanning CNN, transformer, and vision-language model families.
  • Cross-state generalization evaluation for real-world model transfer.
